# Expense Policy — Northwind Labs Finance

> Fictional document written for this portfolio project. Northwind Labs is a
> made-up company; nothing here describes a real employer's systems.

## Submitting an expense report

Submit expense reports in ExpenseHub within 30 days of the purchase date.
Attach a receipt for every expense over $25. Reports are approved by your
manager first, then reviewed by Finance. Approved reports are reimbursed
within 10 business days to your payroll bank account.

## Meal limits

Meals are reimbursed up to $75 per day when travelling for work, and up to
$25 per day for team lunches in the office. Alcohol is never reimbursable.
Keep itemised receipts for all meals; credit card statements alone are not
accepted.

## Travel expenses

Book flights and hotels through the Northwind travel portal to get the
negotiated corporate rates. Economy flights are standard; business class
requires VP approval. Mileage for personal car use is reimbursed at $0.67
per mile. Rental cars must be booked in the compact or mid-size category.

## What is not reimbursable

Personal entertainment, fines, traffic tickets, and expenses for family
members are not reimbursable. If you are unsure whether an expense is
covered, ask Finance at finance@northwind.example before you spend it.
