# VPN Access — Northwind Labs IT Help Center

> Fictional document written for this portfolio project. Northwind Labs is a
> made-up company; nothing here describes a real employer's systems.

## Getting VPN access

To work remotely, you need VPN access to the Northwind office network.
First, request access by emailing vpn-requests@northwind.example with your
manager copied in. Access is usually approved within one business day.

## Installing the VPN client

Once approved, download the Northwind VPN client from
vpn.northwind.example/download and install it on your laptop. Sign in with
your work email and password, then approve the multi-factor authentication
(MFA) prompt in the Northwind Authenticator app. The client is available
for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

## Connecting from home

Open the VPN client and click "Connect". Choose the server closest to your
location — US-East, US-West, or EU-Central. A successful connection shows
a green status icon and your office IP address. If the connection drops,
try switching servers, then restart the VPN client before contacting IT.

## Troubleshooting VPN problems

If the VPN client will not connect, check that your internet connection is
working, that you have the latest version of the client installed, and that
your password has not expired (passwords expire every 90 days). If you see
error code VPN-101, your access has expired and you need to re-request it
from vpn-requests@northwind.example. For anything else, contact the IT help
desk at helpdesk@northwind.example or extension 4357.
