# Password Reset — Northwind Labs IT Help Center

> Fictional document written for this portfolio project. Northwind Labs is a
> made-up company; nothing here describes a real employer's systems.

## How to reset your password

If you forgot your password or your account is locked, go to the Northwind
self-service password portal at password.northwind.example and click
"Forgot password". Enter your work email address and follow the link sent
to your inbox. The reset link expires after 30 minutes.

## Password rules

Your new password must be at least 12 characters long and include one
uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special
character. Passwords expire every 90 days, and you cannot reuse any of
your previous five passwords.

## Locked accounts

After five failed login attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes.
If you are still locked out after 15 minutes, or you no longer have access
to the email address on file, contact the IT help desk at
helpdesk@northwind.example or extension 4357 and ask for a manual password
reset. Have your employee ID ready.

## Multi-factor authentication

Password resets require multi-factor authentication (MFA). Approve the
push notification in the Northwind Authenticator app, or enter the
six-digit code from the app, to complete the reset.
